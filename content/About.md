# About This Project

## Who Made This?

This website was created by **Dr. Prajwal Dange, MBBS, MS, DNB, MCh Head Neck Surgery and Oncology (AIIMS Raipur) (Gold Medalist)**, to help fellow students preparing for the **DNB ENT Theory Examination**.

## Why This Exists

Preparing for DNB ENT theory exams is challenging. Question papers from previous years are scattered, and finding well-referenced answers is time-consuming. This project aims to solve that by providing:

- **All questions** from DNB ENT Theory papers (**2011–2025**)
- **Organised by topic** and subspecialty for focused study
- **Referenced answers** based on **Scott-Brown's Otorhinolaryngology, Head and Neck Surgery, 8th Edition**
- **Free and open access** for all DNB ENT students

## How to Use This Site

1. **Browse by Paper** — Questions are organised under Paper 1 (Otology), Paper 2 (Rhinology), Paper 3 (Larynx & Head Neck), and Paper 4 (General)
2. **Browse by Topic** — Each paper is further divided into subtopics for focused study
3. **Use the Search** — The search bar at the top lets you find any topic instantly
4. **Check High-Yield Topics** — The [[index|landing page]] has a table of the most frequently asked topics
5. **Use the Graph View** — The interactive graph shows connections between related topics

## The Numbers

- **219** answer files
- **883** individual questions covered
- **4** papers across **15 years** (2011–2025)
- **1** reference textbook (Scott-Brown 8th Edition)
