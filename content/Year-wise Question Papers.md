# Year-wise Question Papers (2011–2025)

This page provides a year-wise overview of the DNB ENT Theory examination. All 883 questions across 4 papers from 2011 to 2025 are covered in the answer files linked from the [[index|main index]].

---

## Exam Pattern

The DNB ENT Theory examination consists of **4 papers**, each of 3 hours duration:

| Paper | Subject | Key Areas |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| **Paper 1** | Otology | Anatomy, Audiology, Middle Ear, Vestibular, Facial Nerve, Skull Base |
| **Paper 2** | Rhinology & Oral Cavity | Nasal Anatomy, FESS, Sinusitis, Skull Base, Salivary Glands, Oral Cancer |
| **Paper 3** | Larynx & Head Neck | Laryngeal Cancer, Voice, Thyroid, Swallowing, Neck Dissection, Airway |
| **Paper 4** | General Topics | Research, Imaging, Chemotherapy, Reconstruction, Wound Healing, Robotics |

---

## Year-wise Trends

### Question Volume Over the Years

The DNB ENT exam has evolved significantly from 2011 to 2025:

- **2011–2016** — Earlier papers with a smaller question pool. Core topics like CSOM, FESS, and Laryngeal Cancer were heavily tested.
- **2017–2019** — Increasing diversity in topics. Research Methodology and newer technologies started appearing.
- **2020–2022** — Post-COVID surge in questions on Fungal Rhinosinusitis and Mucormycosis. Significant increase in technology-related questions (NBI, Navigation, Robotic Surgery).
- **2023–2025** — Questions becoming more clinical and scenario-based. Immunotherapy, molecular markers, and recent advances are now regularly tested.

## Most Repeated Topics Across Years

These topics have appeared **almost every year** from 2011 to 2025:

### Paper 1 — Otology
- Audiology tests (BERA, OAE, Impedance) — asked every session
- Cochlear Implant — appears yearly
- CSOM and Cholesteatoma management
- Meniere's Disease and Vestibular disorders
- Facial Nerve palsy and surgery

### Paper 2 — Rhinology & Oral Cavity
- FESS — steps and complications (every year)
- Frontal sinus approaches
- Juvenile Nasopharyngeal Angiofibroma (JNA)
- CSF Rhinorrhea
- Oral Cavity SCC and OSMF
- Salivary gland tumours (Parotid)

### Paper 3 — Larynx & Head Neck
- Laryngeal Carcinoma staging and management
- Conservation Laryngeal Surgery
- Spasmodic Dysphonia (almost every year)
- Neck Dissection classification
- Tracheostomy — indications and complications
- Subglottic Stenosis

### Paper 4 — General
- Research Methodology (Study designs, Bias)
- Wound Healing
- Shock — classification and management
- LASER principles
- NBI and newer imaging
- Reconstruction flaps (PMMC, Radial forearm, Fibula)
